Delete your account & data

How to delete your Tolerize account and the data stored for it. This applies to the app Tolerize (com.joeyapps.tolerize) on Android and iPhone.

Option 1: in the app

Open Account → Delete my account and confirm. It takes effect immediately.

  • If you are the parent your clinic invited, this deletes the household: the dose log, batches, doctor visits, reminder settings and everyone's sign-ins.
  • If someone added you to their household, it deletes only your own sign-in; the household keeps its plan.

Uninstalling the app removes what is stored on your phone.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email us from the address you sign in with:

Request account deletion

Or write to app@tolerize.ca with the subject "Tolerize account deletion request". We confirm the request comes from the account's email and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted, and when

DataWhen
Your sign-in, household members, dose log, batches, visits, reminder settings and push tokens Immediately from the live database; from encrypted backups within 14 days
Server logs (IP addresses)Within 14 days

Kept: a security-log entry recording that the account was deleted, with no health information. Your clinic keeps its own medical records, which this does not affect.

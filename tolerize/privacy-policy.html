Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Tolerize is an oral immunotherapy (OIT) tracker for Android and iPhone. Families use it to follow a child's food immunotherapy plan as their allergist set it: today's dose and how to prepare it, a shared dose log, reminders, and doctor visits. Tolerize is invite-only: an allergy clinic enrols the patient and sends the family an invite. This policy explains what data Tolerize handles, why, where it is kept and who can see it.

Tolerize is provided by JOEY ANDROID APPS, a sole proprietorship in Port Moody, British Columbia, Canada ("we"). Contact: app@tolerize.ca.

Summary

  • Tolerize has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • Your plan and dose log are health information. They are stored encrypted on a server in Canada (Toronto) and shared only with the people in your household and the clinic that enrolled you.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Account → Delete my account).

Tolerize does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or any data from other apps.

Who can see it

  • Your household: everyone you add sees the same plan, dose log, reminders and visits. You can remove someone at any time.
  • Your clinic: the allergist and staff of the clinic that enrolled you see your plan, your account email, and a summary of the dose log (for example the last dose and any skipped days). Front-desk staff see appointments only. Every clinic sign-in and change is logged. The clinic keeps its own medical records separately; this policy does not cover them.
  • Us: only to run, secure and support the service, using individual, logged administrator access.

How it is protected

  • All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • The database is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and kept on a server in Canada. Backups are encrypted the same way and stay in Canada.
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Sign-ins are rate-limited.
  • On the phone, your sign-in and the cached plan are excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

Children

Tolerize is used by parents and caregivers (adults) to manage a child's treatment. Children do not have accounts. A child's treatment information is entered by their clinic and their caregivers.

Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete in the app: Account → Delete my account. For the parent the clinic invited, this deletes your account and the household's dose log, batches, visits, reminder settings and everyone's sign-ins at once. For another household member it removes only their own sign-in. See how to delete your account for other ways.
  • Deleted data leaves the live database immediately and the encrypted backups within 14 days. Server logs (IP addresses) are kept for up to 14 days.
  • A record that an account was deleted, without any health data, is kept in the security audit log.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing app@tolerize.ca. We answer within 30 days. Tolerize is governed by British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act; if you are not satisfied with our answer you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

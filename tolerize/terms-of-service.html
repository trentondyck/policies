1. What Tolerize is

Tolerize helps families follow an oral or sublingual immunotherapy plan that their allergist has prescribed: it shows the current dose and how to prepare it, keeps a shared dose log, and sends reminders. Access is by invitation from a participating clinic. Tolerize is free for patients and families.

2. Not medical advice

Tolerize does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it never changes a dose on its own. Every plan, dose and change comes from your allergist. If what the app shows differs from your allergist's instructions, follow your allergist and tell your clinic. Reminders can fail (for example if notifications are off, a phone is offline or out of battery), so do not rely on the app alone to give a dose on time. In an emergency, use your epinephrine auto-injector and call 911 (or your local emergency number).

Tolerize

Oral immunotherapy tracker. Your allergist sets the plan; Tolerize helps your family keep it.

Tolerize is for families running a child's food oral immunotherapy (OIT) or sublingual immunotherapy (SLIT) under an allergist. The clinic enrols the patient and controls the plan; the app shows today's dose and how to prepare it, keeps a dose log the whole household shares, and rings the right phones when a dose hasn't been logged.

  • Today's dose and recipe, exactly as your allergist set it
  • Press and hold to log the dose; everyone in the household sees it
  • Reminders that escalate to the rest of the household if a dose isn't logged
  • The batch in your fridge: doses left and when to make more
  • Sick-day guidance and skipped-day log
  • Doctor-visit alarms with a what-to-bring list

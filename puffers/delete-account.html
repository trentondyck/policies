Delete your account & data

This page explains how to delete your Puffers account and the data associated with it. It applies to the app Puffers (com.joeyapps.puffers).

Option 1 — remove data in the app

You can delete medications, household members and reference photos directly in the app at any time. Clearing the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Puffers → Storage → Clear data) or uninstalling the app removes the copy stored on your device.

Option 2 — delete your account and all server-stored data

To delete your account and everything stored for it on the Puffers server, send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account (or include your username):

Request account deletion

If the button doesn't open your mail app, email yorkie.joey.joester@gmail.com with the subject "Puffers account deletion request" and your username or sign-in email.

What is deleted

DeletedRetention
Your account (username / Google identity), the households you own, household members you added, all medications, the full dose/puff log, and any reference photos. Removed within 30 days of a verified request.

We keep no analytics or advertising data — there is none to delete. Minimal records may be retained only where required by law.

Questions? Contact yorkie.joey.joester@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Puffers is a shared inhaler dose tracker for Android and iPhone. It helps caregivers log a person's inhaler doses, count the puffs remaining, and coordinate so a dose is never missed or double-given. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Summary

  • Puffers contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party trackers.
  • Sign-in is handled by Google ("Sign in with Google"), Apple ("Sign in with Apple", iPhone) or Firebase Authentication (Google, for email/password accounts) — we never see or store your password.
  • You can use Puffers as a guest, without an account. A guest's medications, doses and photos stay on the phone; the only thing sent to us is the phone's device ID and a few counts (below).
  • Data you create (medications, doses, household members) is stored on your device and, when you sign in, on the app's own server so it can sync to the other phones in your household.
  • Your household's data is visible only to members you approve. It is never sold.

Medication and dose information is health-related personal data. It is collected only because you enter it, and used only to provide the tracking and sharing features of the app.

How data is stored and protected

  • Data is held on your device and on the app's server (operated by the developer). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never reach the app's server.
  • All communication between the app and the server uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Your household's data is accessible only to household members whom the household owner has explicitly approved.

Sharing

Puffers does not sell your data and does not share it for advertising. Data is shared only with the household members you invite and approve, and with Google or Apple, solely to authenticate you, through whichever sign-in you choose.

Children

Puffers is intended for use by adults (parents and caregivers) to manage a dependent's medication. It is not directed to children under 13. A patient profile may include a child's first name, which is entered by the supervising adult.

Your choices and data deletion

  • You can remove medications, members and reference photos in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and all its server data in the app: Settings → Delete this household and my account, or Delete my data on the screen shown when a free trial ends. As a guest, Delete my data removes everything from the phone.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes the copy on your device.
  • To delete your account and the data associated with it on the server, use the account & data deletion page (or email yorkie.joey.joester@gmail.com). Verified requests are actioned within 30 days.
  • The device ID record (the ID, no medication data) and a one-way hash of the account's email are kept after deletion, together with when the free trial started and whether the one-time extra free month was used, so that neither can be restarted by deleting and re-creating an account. The counts are cleared.

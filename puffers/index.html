Puffers

Shared inhaler dose tracker — log every puff, see what's left, never double-dose.

Puffers makes running out of inhaler medication impossible to do by accident, and makes "did the other parent already give it?" a question neither caregiver ever has to ask. It replaces the doctor's paper chart, which was quietly doing two jobs at once — counting puffs and coordinating between caregivers — and does both on every phone in the household.

What you can do

  • Track a scheduled preventer (a breathing dose ring shows puffs left and days remaining, with a per-day strip of which doses are done and by whom) and an as-needed reliever.
  • Add and configure your own medications — name, puffs per canister, how often, who gives each dose, re-prime threshold and refill point.
  • Log priming sprays, undo a mistaken tap, or fix the time on a dose after the fact.
  • Start a new canister on reload — capturing total puffs, purchase and expiry dates, and a photo of the box or label.
  • Get clear warnings before you're caught out: expired, needs priming, running low, or expiring soon — each with a one-tap action.
  • Set up a household and invite the other parent or a carer with a link; you approve who joins.

Puffers is an organisational and reminder tool. It does not provide medical advice — always follow your physician's and pharmacist's instructions.

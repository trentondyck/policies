The Long Haul

Your complete trailer and RV companion for iOS.

The Long Haul is an all-in-one app for trailer and RV owners. Plan trips, log your travels, track maintenance, manage checklists and packing lists, browse campsites on a map, and check the weather — all in one place, with everything stored privately on your device and synced via iCloud.

Trip Planner & Log

Plan upcoming trips with destinations, dates, and notes. Once you're on the road, log your travels and review stats like total distance and nights away.

Maintenance Log

Keep a detailed record of every service, repair, and inspection for your trailer. Set reminders so nothing falls through the cracks.

Checklists & Packing Lists

Build reusable checklists for departure and arrival, and keep a packing list that's ready every time you head out.

Campsite Map

Browse campsites on an interactive map using your current location to find nearby spots.

Weather

Get weather forecasts for any location you're heading to, powered by Apple WeatherKit.

iCloud Sync

All your data syncs privately across your Apple devices via iCloud. No account required — your data stays yours.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

The Long Haul does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All data you enter into the app remains private to you.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, your data is synced privately via Apple's iCloud/CloudKit service. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Photos

Photos you add to the app (trailer photos, note photos, receipt photos, campsite review photos) are stored locally on your device and backed up to your private iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. Photos are never uploaded to any server we operate.

Location

The app requests access to your location (while in use) to show your position on the campsite map. Location data is used only within the app and is never transmitted to any server.

Weather Data

Weather forecasts are provided by Apple WeatherKit. Location names you enter are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to retrieve forecasts. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how this data is handled.

Notifications

The app may send local push notifications for maintenance and date reminders you configure. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and no data is sent to external servers.

Analytics & Advertising

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. No data about your usage is collected.

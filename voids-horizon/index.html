Void's Horizon

Tilt to roll a star through 30 mazes of black holes, ice and gravity storms.

Tilt your phone. Roll your star. Reach the horizon — without falling into the void. Void's Horizon is a tilt-controlled marble maze set at the edge of a black hole. Guide a glowing star across 30 hand-built mazes by physically tilting your device.

Features

  • Pure tilt controls — no buttons needed (an on-screen pad is available if you want it)
  • Five themed collections, 30 mazes, 90 stars to chase
  • Cosmic mechanics: black holes, ice, stardust, timed gates, bumpers, repulsors, wormholes and gravity currents
  • Earn cosmetic skins by playing, or unlock premium sets with optional megapacks
  • Plays fully offline · no intrusive ads · progress saves automatically
